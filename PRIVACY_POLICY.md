# Boson Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 14, 2026

**Contact:** office@waylith.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Boson collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Boson desktop, iOS, or Android application and its related cloud synchronization services.

## 1. Who Operates Boson

Boson is operated by Waylith Solutions LLC ("Waylith," "we," "us," or "our"). Questions or privacy requests may be sent to **office@waylith.com**.

## 2. Information We Collect

Boson collects information that you choose to enter and technical information needed to provide account access, offline storage, cloud synchronization, security, and support.

- Account and profile information, including your name, email address, optional Waylith account profile picture, account identifier, display language, display currency, and appearance preferences.
- Plan and billing information, including the access source, plan, status, duration, billing provider and interval, provider product and transaction or subscription identifiers, a pseudonymous Apple account-association token, renewal, expiration, grace-period, cancellation and revocation state, pending billing attempts, and negotiated capacity limits. For a manually assigned plan, this also includes the authorized operator, reason, creation time, expiration, and revocation history. Boson does not receive your complete payment-card number, Apple Account password, or App Store payment credential.
- Business information, including business names, owner or manager names, business email addresses, business phone numbers, business addresses, currencies, enabled modules, images, and preferences.
- Operational records, including sales, returns, expenses, payment methods, inventory, daily sessions, open tabs, services, invoices, reminders, and audit-history events.
- Appointment information, including titles, scheduled dates and times, time zones, durations, attendees, locations, descriptions, links, text materials, completion or non-completion outcome, optional reminder timing, and the recipient email addresses you choose. If you add appointment files, this also includes their contents, original filenames, detected types, sizes, and SHA-256 integrity checksums.
- Personal-finance tracker information, including manually entered or imported transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, income, expense, or transfer classifications, categories, notes, source filenames, row or page references, clarification choices, and import history.
- Public crypto-wallet tracker information, including one public Bitcoin mainnet, Solana mainnet, or EVM address, Boson's automatically recognized address family, supported balances, public transaction or transfer activity, network fees, provider warnings, fiat valuations, portfolio and 24-hour change calculations, and the times Boson checked the address. A 0x address is checked across Ethereum Mainnet, Base, OP Mainnet, Arbitrum One, and Polygon. Existing records that explicitly identify one EVM network retain that network; older records with no saved network remain assigned to Base. Boson does not request or store a private key, seed phrase, extended wallet key, wallet approval, or signing credential.
- Team-access information, including employee names and email addresses entered by the business owner, permission-group names and settings, non-secret key identifiers, an optional Boson account identifier when an employee saves a workspace to an account, key delivery, regeneration, redemption, and revocation timestamps, invoice-delivery events, and an audit journal of data changes made through employee keys. When you activate employee keys without an account on one device, Boson also keeps limited local display and routing metadata for each activated business, employee, permission group, workspace, and non-secret key identifier.
- Client information that you choose to record, including client names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, notes, service history, invoices, and payment status.
- User-selected images, such as a business image, inventory item image, invoice logo, or invoice watermark.
- Technical and security information, including user and device synchronization identifiers, timestamps, authentication events, IP addresses, user-agent information, request metadata, and error or performance logs generated by service providers.

## 3. Passwords and Authentication

Boson uses Supabase Auth to create and authenticate accounts. Passwords are transmitted securely to Supabase for authentication. Boson does not receive or store your password in readable form. Authentication refresh sessions may be retained in encrypted device storage so that you can remain signed in or return to a remembered profile from the signed-out login screen. You may optionally enable authenticator-app two-step verification; Boson stores cryptographic hashes of your one-time recovery codes, not the readable codes. Employee access keys are bearer credentials generated at the business owner's direction and emailed directly to the employee address the owner provides. The readable key is not shown to the business owner. Boson Cloud stores a one-way hash, the non-secret key identifier, its assigned permissions, its delivery address, and its status. After an employee redeems a key without an account, Boson may retain that Supabase refresh token and limited Activated Keys display metadata in OS-protected encrypted storage on that device. The Activated Keys control appears only on the signed-out login screen and only when at least one key profile is remembered. The vault does not retain an access token, readable employee key, temporary authentication password, or account password. An employee may instead link a key to a normal Boson account to save that business in the account's workspace switcher. In that case, Boson stores the account identifier on the employee-key record but still does not store the readable key.

## 4. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

Boson is local-first: financial and business records are cached on your device so core records remain available while offline. When you are signed in and a connection is available, supported account and business records synchronize automatically with Boson Cloud, which is hosted using Supabase.

Personal-finance and crypto-wallet trackers use a separate owner-only account namespace. Their profiles, display settings, transaction records, import provenance, public addresses, public activity, supported balances, and wallet observations are cached in encrypted device storage and synchronize through Boson Cloud so authorized devices can converge. They are stored as ordinary JSON/JSONB within Boson's provider-managed cloud security boundary and are not end-to-end encrypted from Boson, Supabase, or sufficiently privileged infrastructure administrators. Reproducible public market-price history is fetched when a Crypto overview opens and is kept only in the app's temporary memory for that session; Boson does not write that price history to device storage, Boson Cloud, or backups. While that data is loaded, Boson may derive chart points and 24-hour changes from public prices and observed balances even when no transfer occurred.

Appointments use a separate owner-only account namespace and synchronize through Boson Cloud so the owner's authorized devices can converge. A separate owner-only history projection lets the account display completed and not-completed outcomes for up to 90 days after the scheduled occurrence. Reminder recipients, active appointment content, and eligible history content are stored as ordinary JSON/JSONB. Selected appointment files are stored separately in a private Supabase Storage bucket, and Boson stores server-controlled file metadata with the appointment so authorized devices can list and retrieve them. Appointment records, metadata, and files are protected by Boson's provider-managed cloud security boundary, but they are not end-to-end encrypted from Boson, Supabase, or sufficiently privileged infrastructure administrators. The reminder worker receives the applicable appointment content only when you enable a reminder. If you also choose Include Files, Boson freezes the current file set for that delivery. Validated PNG, JPEG, WebP, and TXT sets up to 10 MiB may be sent as email attachments; other supported types and larger sets use recipient-specific, expiring Waylith download links that stream files from private Storage. Direct email attachments cannot be recalled after provider acceptance. Protected links may be revoked when the appointment is rescheduled, completed, deleted, expires, has its reminder disabled, has Include Files turned off, or no longer includes the applicable file. Marking a past appointment complete or deleting it removes the appointment from the active account record and queues its files for deletion. An uncompleted appointment is removed after it has been past due for three days; rescheduling it to a future time before then restarts that lifecycle from the new occurrence.

Public-wallet checks are scheduled only while Boson is open and able to run and are limited to no more than once per 60 seconds per tracker. Boson does not monitor or refresh a wallet while the app is closed or suspended.

CSV, TSV, delimiter-separated text, and XLSX transaction-data files selected for an import are parsed on the device. Boson does not upload or retain the source file as part of the import. The transaction records, classifications, and provenance created by an import remain in the applicable Business or General Purpose tracker. XML and PDF import are not currently supported.

Boson encrypts the local account cache, queued changes, current authentication session, and remembered-access refresh-session vault with a random device key protected by Windows DPAPI, the iOS Keychain, or the Android Keystore. Remembered profiles and their display metadata remain only on that device; they are not synchronized to Boson Cloud and are excluded from business and complete-account backups. A previously verified trusted owner device may open its existing encrypted cache for a limited period while offline. Accountless employee access does not receive that offline grant: opening an Activated Key requires an internet connection and authoritative revalidation of the session, key, workspace, plan, and current permissions. Changes made offline through an eligible owner session synchronize after the device reconnects and the account is revalidated. If the protected device key is lost, synchronized cloud records can be downloaded again, but changes that existed only on that device and remembered-access profiles may be unrecoverable.

Boson keeps an encrypted backup library on each device. Automatic backup is enabled by default for each business and is scheduled once per day at 3:00 AM device time; if Boson is closed or suspended then, it catches up the next time the app opens or resumes. Automatic-backup settings are device-specific. Automatic entries are deleted after seven days. Manual business backups, manual complete-account backups, and pre-restore safety entries do not expire automatically, but they remain local app data and can be deleted by you or lost if the app or device storage is removed. A complete-account backup includes owned business workspaces, account preferences, the local invoice-design library, General Purpose and Crypto tracker profiles and records, active Appointments, and eligible Appointment History; a single-business backup does not include account-level trackers, Appointments, Appointment History, or designs. Appointment file contents and server-controlled attachment, reminder, delivery, and deletion projections are excluded from every `.bosonbackup` payload. The local catalog retains limited routing and retention metadata such as account and workspace identifiers, backup type, and timestamps. Names, records, and summary counts remain inside the encrypted payload.

Custom invoice schematics and copied invoice artwork are stored in encrypted local account storage so a source image can be moved or deleted without breaking the design. Complete account backups include that device library; single-business backups do not. When you choose to send an invoice, the selected design and necessary invoice, business, and client information are transmitted to Boson Cloud and the email provider for that delivery.

You may export a library entry as an encrypted `.bosonbackup` file. On iOS and Android, Boson writes the file to its Documents area—under `Documents/Boson` on Android—and offers the system sharing interface; exported files may also be included in device or cloud backups according to your platform settings. Boson Cloud stores the small per-account encryption key needed to open backups, but does not store a copy or history of backup payloads. Restoring requires the same active account, your current password, and any enabled two-step verification. A confirmed single-business restore replaces only that business. A complete-account backup replaces General Purpose and Crypto tracker data, active Appointments, or Appointment History only when the applicable versioned section is present. An older complete-account backup with no such section preserves the account's newer data for that feature. An appointment restore does not recreate, roll back, or recover appointment files: an existing cloud file may remain associated when its same appointment record ID survives the restore, while a file already deleted or cleaned from Storage cannot be recovered from the backup. Because backups also exclude service-side reminder-delivery history, restoring an Appointments section preserves its reminder settings and recipients but suppresses those restored reminders. To resume delivery, change the occurrence, time zone, or reminder lead time; alternatively, save the reminder disabled and enable it in a later edit. Editing only details or recipients does not resume delivery.

Synchronized information is associated with the owner's Boson account and business. Boson uses owner-based and permission-group access controls so an owner may grant a named employee key limited access to one business. The owner supplies the employee email used for direct key delivery and replacement-key delivery. An accountless employee may activate keys for multiple businesses on one device, but each remembered session remains limited to its independently issued key and one workspace. Saved access can be selected only from the signed-out login screen, and selecting a saved key always revalidates it online before opening cached business data. For a remembered profile, Manual Sign Out clears the app's current sign-in state and automatic-reopen marker on that device while preserving the encrypted saved profile and its refresh credential for later login-screen use. It does not invoke provider logout or revoke the retained refresh credential. A session without a remembered profile uses normal provider sign-out. Remove From This Device deletes only the selected profile from that device's encrypted vault; it does not revoke, regenerate, or delete the employee key in Boson Cloud. Only the business owner controls those backend key actions. A signed-in employee may alternatively save memberships from multiple businesses to one account, but every workspace remains independently limited by the key and permission group issued by that business; saving or remembering access does not transfer ownership. Employees can read and change only the collections and actions permitted by their current group, and the owner can regenerate or revoke a key. Regeneration and revocation stop access through the prior credential or linked account membership; a remembered credential that no longer passes revalidation is removed, but neither action can remotely erase business records already cached on an employee-controlled device. Joined and accountless employee workspaces are excluded from owner portfolio analytics and account backups. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted from Boson or its infrastructure providers.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use collected information only as reasonably necessary to:

- Create, authenticate, secure, and support your account.
- Resolve and administer the features and capacity available to your account across supported platforms.
- Verify and reconcile subscriptions, prevent a second subscription from being started through another billing channel, process renewals, cancellations, refunds, revocations, and restores, and maintain billing audit and idempotency records.
- Provide business, personal-finance, read-only public-wallet, and appointment tracking, including the optional reminders, reporting, invoices, backups, preferences, and other requested app functionality.

- Maintain data integrity, reconcile records, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and protect the service.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

## 6. Service Providers and Disclosures

We do not sell personal information, use it for cross-app tracking, or use it for third-party advertising. We disclose information only to service providers that help operate Boson, when you direct us to do so, or when required by law.

- **Supabase** provides authentication, database hosting, synchronization APIs, private Storage for appointment files, operational logging, the private account-backup key, and narrowly scoped account-restoration and account-deletion functions. Supabase infrastructure may also use providers such as Cloudflare for network delivery and security.
- **Resend** provides transactional email for account messages, employee access keys, support responses, invoices, and appointment reminders you direct Boson to send. For employee-key delivery, Resend receives the employee email address and name, business name, message subject, and readable access credential needed to transmit the message. For invoice delivery, Resend receives the recipient email address, business Reply-To address, subject, invoice contents, and selected visual design. For an appointment reminder, Resend receives one selected recipient address per provider request plus the appointment title, schedule, time zone, duration, description, location, attendees, links, and text materials that apply to the message. When you choose Include Files, Resend also receives either the applicable file contents, names, and types as email attachments or the file names and protected Waylith download links when link delivery is used. Resend may process ordinary delivery metadata such as provider IDs, timestamps, failures, bounces, IP addresses, and message events.
- **Cloudflare** hosts Waylith's protected appointment-file download route. For a linked reminder file, Cloudflare receives an opaque download token and file identifier and may process ordinary network information such as an IP address, user agent, and request logs. The route does not receive a Supabase service-role key or expose the private Storage path to the recipient.
- **Frankfurter** provides exchange-rate information. Boson sends requested currency codes, not your business records, but the provider may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address and user agent.
- **Blockscout's public explorer APIs**, including Blockscout's public Base explorer API, provide public balances, token information, transaction and transfer activity, provider risk labels, and available market-rate information for supported EVM networks. For a 0x address, Boson sends the address and read-only query parameters to the Ethereum Mainnet, Base, OP Mainnet, Arbitrum One, and Polygon instances so the user does not have to identify a network.
- **Blockstream's public Bitcoin API** provides the balance and public transaction history for the exact Bitcoin mainnet address entered. It does not identify or retrieve other rotating addresses that may belong to the same wallet.
- **Solana's public mainnet RPC and PublicNode's public Solana RPC** provide the native SOL balance and public signature or transaction activity for the exact Solana address entered. Boson may send the same read-only request to PublicNode automatically when Solana's public endpoint is unavailable or refuses the request.
- **Coinbase's unauthenticated public price endpoint** provides available Bitcoin and Solana spot-price information. Boson does not connect to, read, or control a Coinbase account in this implementation.
- For these public-wallet checks, Boson sends the pasted public address and read-only query parameters to the applicable provider. The providers may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address and user agent. Although an address and its on-chain activity are public, associating that address with a Boson account or tracker name may be sensitive.
- **IPWhois.io** provides the default business time zone inferred from the business owner’s public IP address. Boson requests only the time-zone result and stores the returned IANA time-zone name with the business profile; it does not store the detected IP address, city, or coordinates.
- **Stripe** processes subscriptions started through Waylith. Stripe receives the account and checkout information needed to create and manage the billing customer and subscription, such as the account email, selected plan and interval, billing details, provider customer and subscription identifiers, payment status, and ordinary fraud-prevention and network metadata. Boson receives subscription status and identifiers, not full payment-card credentials.
- **Apple** processes information when you download or test the iOS app through the App Store or TestFlight and when you view, buy, restore, renew, manage, cancel, or request a refund for an iOS subscription. Apple receives the selected App Store product, a pseudonymous UUID that associates the transaction with the signed-in Boson account, transaction and subscription information, and ordinary App Store account, device, storefront, payment, fraud-prevention, and network information under Apple's privacy terms. Boson receives signed transaction and renewal information, localized product presentation, provider identifiers, status, expiration, revocation, and notification data; it does not receive your Apple Account password or App Store payment credential.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Account, synchronized business information, synchronized General Purpose and Crypto tracker records, active Appointments, and their live files are retained while your Boson account remains active or as needed to provide the service. A remembered-access profile remains in encrypted storage on that device until you choose Remove From This Device, remove or reset the app's local storage, or Boson removes it after a definitive failed revalidation. For a remembered profile, Manual Sign Out clears ordinary sign-in storage and the automatic-reopen marker on that device but preserves the encrypted profile and its refresh credential; it does not invoke provider logout or revoke that credential. A session without a remembered profile uses normal provider sign-out. Removing a profile from one device does not revoke or delete its employee key in Boson Cloud; the business owner controls the backend key separately. Removing a General Purpose or Crypto tracker synchronizes its deletion to the account's other authorized devices; removing a Crypto tracker does not affect the public wallet or blockchain. Completed or eligible manually deleted Appointments are removed from the active record and may appear with their outcome in Appointment History; uncompleted Appointments are automatically removed from the active record three days after their scheduled occurrence unless first moved to a future time. Appointment History content is removed from device caches and scrubbed from the cloud no later than 90 days after the scheduled occurrence. The cloud may retain a minimal record identifier and deletion/synchronization metadata after that scrub solely to keep stale devices from restoring deleted content. Appointment file objects are deleted asynchronously with retry so a temporary Storage failure does not silently abandon deletion. After Storage deletion is confirmed, limited terminal file metadata—including the original filename, detected type, size, SHA-256 checksum, internal object path, identifiers, and deletion timestamps—may remain for no more than 90 days and is capped to the newest 10,000 terminal entries per account so Boson can prove and safely retry lifecycle operations. Limited reminder-delivery and security metadata may also remain when reasonably necessary to enforce delivery limits, prevent duplicate messages, diagnose failures, or protect the service. A separate private reminder-budget reservation may retain only an internal delivery ID, account ID, recipient-address SHA-256 hash, and timestamp for no more than 48 hours so account or appointment deletion cannot immediately reset sender-abuse limits; it contains no appointment content, readable recipient address, or file data. You can permanently delete your account from Account Settings. Account deletion first removes the account's appointment file objects, then detaches memberships that the account saved as an employee, revokes and deletes employee authentication users issued by businesses the account owns, removes the owner account and its owned synchronized workspaces, General Purpose and Crypto tracker cloud records, active Appointments, Appointment History, reminder-recipient records, plan-entitlement sources, team-access configuration, employee audit journal, and records from Boson Cloud, and removes that account's local Boson namespace from the device where deletion is completed. Deleting an employee's linked account does not delete a business owned by someone else.

Copies that you exported, device backups, or caches on other devices remain under your control and may need to be removed separately. Permanently deleting the Boson account also deletes its server-held backup encryption key, so secure account-backup files for that account can no longer be restored through Boson. Limited operational logs, security records, or provider backups may remain for a defined retention period when reasonably necessary for security, disaster recovery, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Deleting your Boson account does not automatically cancel an Apple or Stripe subscription. Cancel it through the provider before deleting the account. Apple, Stripe, and their financial partners may retain transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legal-compliance records under their own policies and obligations.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and edit many account, business, personal-finance, public-wallet, appointment, and client records in Boson; create, reschedule, complete, or delete an Appointment; enable or disable its optional email reminder, choose its recipients, and choose whether to include files; remove a General Purpose or Crypto tracker from your synchronized account; manage permission groups; update employee delivery addresses; regenerate or revoke employee keys; review and, where conflict-safe, undo employee data changes; save or restore an encrypted local business or complete-account backup; restore or manage an eligible Apple subscription in the iOS app; manage a Waylith subscription through its billing portal; select remembered access from the signed-out login screen; manually sign out to stop automatic reopening while preserving the local saved profile; use Remove From This Device to forget only that local profile without changing its backend key; and delete your account from Account Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

## 9. Security

Boson uses encrypted network connections, authenticated encryption for local account data and account-backup files, owner-based, employee-permission, and MFA-aware database access controls, one-way employee-key hashes, optional two-step verification, restricted direct-database access, and server-only administrative credentials for narrowly scoped key issuance, account recovery, and deletion operations. No system can guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for protecting your password, employee keys, devices, recovery codes, and exported backups.

## 10. International Processing and Children

Boson and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Boson's current Supabase project is hosted in the Asia-Pacific region. Where required, we rely on service-provider contractual protections for international processing.

Boson is intended for adults managing financial information and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law. Do not create an account for a child or knowingly enter a child's personal information without appropriate legal authority.
