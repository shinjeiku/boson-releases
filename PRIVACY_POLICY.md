# Boson Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 16, 2026  
**Contact:** soap.official@outlook.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Boson collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Boson desktop or iOS application and its related cloud synchronization services.

## 1. Who Operates Boson

Boson is operated by Jacob Jennette ("Boson," "we," "us," or "our"). Questions or privacy requests may be sent to **soap.official@outlook.com**.

## 2. Information We Collect

Boson collects information that you choose to enter and technical information needed to provide account access, offline storage, cloud synchronization, security, and support.

- Account and profile information, including your name, email address, account identifier, display language, display currency, and appearance preferences.
- Business information, including business names, owner or manager names, business email addresses, currencies, enabled modules, images, and preferences.
- Operational records, including sales, returns, expenses, payment methods, inventory, daily sessions, open tabs, services, invoices, reminders, and audit-history events.
- Client information that you choose to record, including client names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, notes, service history, invoices, and payment status.
- User-selected images, such as a business image or inventory item image.
- Technical and security information, including user and device synchronization identifiers, timestamps, authentication events, IP addresses, user-agent information, request metadata, and error or performance logs generated by service providers.

## 3. Passwords and Authentication

Boson uses Supabase Auth to create and authenticate accounts. Passwords are transmitted securely to Supabase for authentication. Boson does not receive or store your password in readable form. Authentication sessions may be retained on your device so that you can remain signed in.

## 4. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

Boson is local-first: business records are cached on your device so core records remain available while offline. When you are signed in and a connection is available, account and business records synchronize automatically with Boson Cloud, which is hosted using Supabase.

Synchronized information is associated with your Boson account. Boson uses owner-based access controls so one account cannot read another account's workspaces through the application. Cloud data is not end-to-end encrypted from Boson or its infrastructure providers.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use collected information only as reasonably necessary to:

- Create, authenticate, secure, and support your account.
- Provide business tracking, reporting, invoices, backups, preferences, and other requested app functionality.
- Synchronize and restore your records across authorized devices.
- Maintain data integrity, reconcile records, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and protect the service.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

## 6. Service Providers and Disclosures

We do not sell personal information, use it for cross-app tracking, or use it for third-party advertising. We disclose information only to service providers that help operate Boson, when you direct us to do so, or when required by law.

- **Supabase** provides authentication, database hosting, synchronization APIs, operational logging, and the account-deletion function. Supabase infrastructure may also use providers such as Cloudflare for network delivery and security.
- **Frankfurter** provides exchange-rate information. Boson sends requested currency codes, not your business records, but the provider may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address and user agent.
- **Apple** processes information when you download or test the iOS app through the App Store or TestFlight, including information governed by Apple's own privacy terms.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Account and synchronized business information is retained while your Boson account remains active or as needed to provide the service. You can permanently delete your account from Personal Settings. Account deletion removes the account and its associated synchronized workspaces and records from Boson Cloud and removes that account's local Boson namespace from the device where deletion is completed.

Copies that you exported, device backups, or caches on other devices remain under your control and may need to be removed separately. Limited operational logs, security records, or provider backups may remain for a defined retention period when reasonably necessary for security, disaster recovery, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and edit many account, business, and client records in Boson; export a local business backup; sign out to stop the current device session; and delete your account from Personal Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

## 9. Security

Boson uses encrypted network connections, authenticated requests, owner-based database access controls, and server-only administrative credentials for account deletion. No system can guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for protecting your password, devices, and exported backups.

## 10. International Processing and Children

Boson and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Boson's current Supabase project is hosted in the Asia-Pacific region. Where required, we rely on service-provider contractual protections for international processing.

Boson is intended for business operators and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law. Do not create an account for a child or knowingly enter a child's personal information without appropriate legal authority.

## 11. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy as Boson changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on the canonical policy page.

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to **soap.official@outlook.com**.
